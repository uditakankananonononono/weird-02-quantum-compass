Rank 2: Candidate (a) — Selection-Regime Shift (RELAX / Codeml Branch-Site)

Why it ranks second: dN/dS ratios assess codon-level selection regimes rather than amino acid physical/structural canalization. RELAX (HyPhy) specifically tests for selection intensification (k>1) or relaxation (k<1) along specified target branches (e.g., Cry1 avian clade vs. Cry2 avian clade, or migratory lineages within Cry1).

Falsifiable Win/Loss Conditions:

Falsification/Loss: Selection intensity parameter k≈1 (no shift) or k<1 (relaxed selection relative to Cry2 background).
