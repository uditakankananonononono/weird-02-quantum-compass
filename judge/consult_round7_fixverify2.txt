1. The central negative constraint is still limited by model-class coverage (moderate–major)

species-specific hyperfine tensors,

radical-pair geometry/orientation,

protein conformational ensembles,

solvent/reorganization effects.

A skeptical judge could argue that the negative result is a failure of model completeness rather than a strong mechanistic constraint.

Tested degree of freedom	Included?	Evidence

electron-transfer pathway changes	no	limitation

This does not need to rescue the ordering; it only defines the boundary.

The result is a strong contradiction of the frozen model for those three points, but it is not a broad statistical estimate of avian magnetosensitivity.

Make the primary evidence hierarchy:

mechanistic falsification of a predefined ordering prediction;

Avoid language where the CI is interpreted like a population estimate.
