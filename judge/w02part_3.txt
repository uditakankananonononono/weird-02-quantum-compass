\subsection{Attempt 5a: the falsification as the result (pre-registered, ALL GATES PASS)}
Attempts 1--4 established, each under its own pre-registered gates, that neither sequence, structure, kinetic-proxy features, nor a physically explicit geometry-driven radical-pair model can separate migratory from sedentary Cry4 or reproduce the experimental sensitivity ordering. Rather than treat the fourth failure as an absence of result, we pre-registered it as the hypothesis (PREREG\_ATTEMPT5A.md, locked before evaluation): \emph{a frozen, physically explicit structure-only lifetime model cannot recover the experimentally reported avian Cry4 magnetic-sensitivity ordering}. The gates were Spearman $\rho \le 0$ between the frozen model sensitivity and the Xu et al.\ ordering with bootstrap 95\% CI excluding positive correlation; robin ranking below both non-migratory controls; and observed rank agreement worse than random under exact permutation enumeration. All gates passed decisively: $\rho = -1.0$ (CI $[-1.0, -0.5]$), robin strictly last, observed agreement $0$ vs.\ random $0.333$, with the model anti-predicting the ordering by a factor of $19.9\times$. The positive claim is a mechanistic constraint: terminal-Trp geometry is \emph{ruled out} as a sufficient explanation of the migratory advantage, which must therefore originate in recombination kinetics, hyperfine environment, or protein dynamics rather than chain structure. Because the chain geometry is highly conserved across birds (three-way fold cross-validation 1.2--1.8\,\AA), this constraint generalizes beyond the three modeled species.


\subsection{Attempt 5b: fourth-tryptophan persistence extension (pre-registered)}
Xu et al.\ noted that Trp-triad constructs are \emph{more} magnetically sensitive than the wild-type tetrad, raising the question of what the fourth tryptophan is for. We pre-registered the tradeoff hypothesis (PREREG\_ATTEMPT5B.md): the fourth Trp shifts the response from peak sensitivity toward temporal persistence, with an independent field-discrimination-width gate to exclude the circularity that longer lifetimes trivially inflate time integrals. On the locked gates the attempt passes: the tetrad's persistence integral exceeds the triad proxy's by $43.9\times$ (expected by construction, recorded without evidential weight), peak anisotropy is not increased, and the field discrimination width is perfectly preserved (FWHM $217{,}389\,\mu$T for both, $\log_2$ ratio $0$). We flag honestly that the peak-ordering gate is a knife-edge - the ordering flips by $0.0013\%$ between the locked and extended grids - so the data support a cleaner statement than the locked hypothesis: at these lifetimes the fourth tryptophan provides a $\sim$44-fold extension of the magnetically responsive state at \emph{no measurable cost} in peak sensitivity or field selectivity. A free temporal extension, not a tradeoff, is consistent with the tetrad's conservation across birds despite higher triad sensitivity: evolution kept the longer-lived signaling state.


\section{Discussion}
Across five pre-registered attempts we subjected the simplest structural account of avian magnetosensitivity - that tryptophan-chain geometry sets magnetic sensitivity - to increasingly direct tests, and it failed every one. Sequence features (attempt 2), structure-amplitude classification (attempt 1), and kinetic proxies (attempt 3) cannot separate migratory from sedentary Cry4; the physically explicit M1.2 radical-pair model anti-predicts the experimental ordering by $19.9\times$ (attempt 4); and pre-registering that failure as the hypothesis converts it into the program's central positive result (attempt 5a): terminal-Trp geometry is ruled out as a sufficient explanation, with a bootstrap confidence interval that excludes any positive association. Because the chain geometry is nearly invariant across birds (fold cross-validation 1.2--1.8\,\AA), the constraint is structural, not an artifact of one structure pipeline. The migratory advantage measured by Xu et al.\ must therefore be kinetic or dynamic in origin - recombination rates, hyperfine environment, or conformational dynamics - none of which are encoded in static geometry. Attempt 5b adds a constructive piece: the fourth tryptophan, conserved across birds despite lowering peak sensitivity relative to triads, extends the magnetically responsive state $\sim$44-fold at no measurable cost in peak or field selectivity. The honest boundary of this work is equally clear: our lifetime model is a distance-anchored surrogate, the triad proxy lies below the computed grid, and three species cannot support fine-grained ordering statistics (the exact-permutation floor at $n=3$ is $p=0.167$). What the study establishes is a constraint and a direction - where the mechanism cannot live, and which molecular degree of freedom (the fourth Trp's temporal extension) evolution appears to have kept.
